# Research plan

Scope: NCAA Division I men's basketball, five completed seasons 2021–22 through
2025–26, real player-school-season entries and shooting/dribbling/defense/speed/
stamina game ratings. Source facts and derived ratings must remain distinct.
Exact special tier odds: HOF 1/800, All-Star 1/100, mutually exclusive. Exactly
eight historical HOF players; four non-college players are purchase-only.
All-Star is the user's latest name for the earlier All-NBA 100–105 tier.

Source classes: official NCAA/school statistics and historical bios; open,
provenanced SportsDataverse datasets as bulk evidence; official pages for spot
checks. Never infer complete coverage from a current-team directory.

1. Complete — discover roster/stat datasets and record coverage/licensing gaps.
2. Complete — follow up missing evidence, reconcile sources, define reproducible ratings.
3. Complete — synthesize research; generate Luau data and configuration only.
4. Complete (data/config verification; full individual scouting remains incomplete) — verify invariants, exact odds, exclusions, source counts and game build.
